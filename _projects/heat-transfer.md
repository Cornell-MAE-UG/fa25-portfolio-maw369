I chose Homework 2 for this portfolio assignment because it helped me to understand how heat transfer concepts can apply to real life situations. The first question in the assignment where we had to look at heat loss from a water heater allowed me to see, through the calculation, how adding insulation changes the amount of power needed to keep the water at a constant temperature. Seeing the result of this problem and comparing the heat loss before and after adding insulation highlighted how much just adding insulation can reduce the power required for heating. This result made understanding how convection and conduction work together in a thermal system a lot more intuitive, and showed how small design changes can make systems a lot more efficient.

The second question where we had to analyzing a thin film heater and determine the maximum heat flux before the heater would overheat also helped to show me how engineers use heat transfer calculations to design systems that can operate safely. I think this homework definitely helped me become more comfortable setting up heat transfer problems, drawing schematics, and applying the equations we have been doing in class to real life systems, and I think these skills will be useful in the future when working in the field.
