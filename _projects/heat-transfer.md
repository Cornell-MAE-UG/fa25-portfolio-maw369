I chose Homework 2 for this portfolio assignment beacuse it helped me to understand how heat transfer concepts can apply to real life situations. The first question in the assignment where we had to look at heat loss from a water heater and got to see through the calculation how adding insulation changes the amount of power needed to keep the water at a constant temperature. Working through this problem and seeing the result really helped me understand how convection and con duction work together in a thermal system. When I compared the heat loss before and after adding insulation, I saw just how much insulation can reduce the required heating power. This made the idea of thermal resistance a lot more intuitive and showed how small design changes can make systems much more efficient.

The second question where we had to analyzing a thin film heater and determine the maximum heat flux before the heater would overheat also helped to show me how engineers use heat transfer calculations to design systems that can operate safely. I think this homework definitely helped me become more comfortable setting up heat transfer problems, drawing schematics, and applying the equations we have been doing in class to real life systems, and I think these skills will be useful in the future when working in the field.
